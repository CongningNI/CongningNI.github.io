Research agenda

AI systems that connect evidence, communication, and care

My work combines computational methods with clinically and socially grounded questions. The three areas below form one research program rather than separate application silos.

01

Caregiver support and communication

Characterizing caregiver needs and designing AI tools that help people organize concerns, prepare questions, and navigate longitudinal care.

  • ADRD caregiver narratives and online communities
  • Peer, clinician, and AI response comparison
  • Caregiver-facing copilots and retrieval-augmented systems

02

Clinical risk and multimodal evidence

Integrating EHRs, clinical notes, patient-reported data, and other longitudinal signals to identify care-related risks earlier.

  • Cancer medication discontinuation
  • Structured and unstructured clinical data integration
  • Interpretable prediction and evidence characterization

03

Trustworthy LLMs for healthcare

Evaluating and aligning large language models so they behave safely, transparently, and consistently in sensitive health settings.

  • Hallucination, omission, and factual grounding
  • Clinical summarization and preference alignment
  • Mental health and patient-facing AI evaluation
